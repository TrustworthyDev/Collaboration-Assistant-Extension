Collaboration Assistant

Click the To, Subject or Message box of an email and a Paste button appears. Clicking it inserts the text you set here. Or click Auto Fill in the email's title bar to fill all three at once. Changes save automatically.

Presets

Leave a box empty to hide the Paste button on that field.

Separate several recipients with commas.
Write a link as [Exploring a Remote Work](https://docs.google.com/…): the email shows only the title, as a clickable link. When you paste text copied from an email, its links are turned into this form for you.

Email list

Addresses you plan to write to, one per line. Each Paste in a To box, or each Auto Fill, uses the next one and moves down the list. While the list has unused addresses, it takes the place of the To text above.

Sites

The button works on email compose forms on any site: any page with a To box plus a Subject or message box. List sites below to switch it off there (subdomains included), one per line. You can also switch the current site off from the toolbar popup.

Advanced: custom field selectors

Fields are detected automatically from their labels. If a field on your mail site is missed, enter a CSS selector for it (or for its wrapper), e.g. #message-to-field or [aria-label="Message body"].
